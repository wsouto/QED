# Glossary

Terms and concepts used in @raicher's Bitcoin analysis.

### QED

**Also known as:** Quod Erat Demonstratum

Latin for "which was to be demonstrated." Raicher's signature when price hits a predicted target. Used as a closing marker on successful trades and predictions.

> *""QED, fechamos com uma vela HORROROSA...""*

### Agulhada

**Also known as:** Agulhadas, Needle

The crossing of 3 moving averages at the same chart point. An agulhada doesn't have a direction — it's just a point. Connecting agulhadas reveals hidden trend lines that aren't visible otherwise.

> *""a agulhada clássica""*

### Flechas (Arrows)

**Also known as:** Flechinha, Colored arrows

Colored markers on charts indicating cycle phases: Yellow (start), Orange (1st impulse), Blue (2nd impulse), Red (top), Green (bear market). Each corresponds to specific RSI levels.

> *""estamos na altura da 2ª flecha laranja""*

### Nostreidamos

Portuguese portmanteau roughly meaning "we will prove it." His analysis brand. Also the name of his Telegram channel where high-res charts are shared.

> *""Raicher's Gulch — Versão em alta resolução exclusiva pro Telegram""*

### Iniciante™

**Also known as:** iniciante

Portuguese for "beginner." His self-deprecating brand. Used as a sign-off on analysis posts ("GN, iniciantes™"). Contrasts with the advanced nature of the analysis.

> *""GN, iniciantes™""*

### Below-Below-Below

**Also known as:** BBB, abaixo-abaixo-abaixo

A multi-timeframe RSI strategy where RSI is below key levels across 3 timeframes simultaneously, creating a high-probability reversal setup.

> *""Lembra da estratégia BELOW-BELOW-BELOW, iniciante?""*

### Raicher Strategy 2

**Also known as:** RS2

A custom indicator developed by Raicher for monthly chart analysis. First mentioned in October 2024. Identifies purple arrow signals on the monthly timeframe.

> *""segundo o meu indicador (Raicher Strategy 2)""*

### BBW

**Also known as:** Bollinger Band Width

Bollinger Bands Width indicator. Used to measure volatility contraction and expansion. Low BBW = compression phase → expected expansion.

> *""Lembram quando fiz essa previsão baseada no BBW em Julho?""*

### Linha Solar

**Also known as:** Solar Line

A trend line derived from connecting specific candle wicks ("agulhadas"). Projects future price levels. Mentioned in Dec 2024 projecting BTC to 130K.

> *""Caso o Bitcoin ganhe de volta a Linha Solar ☀️""*

### Linha Azul

**Also known as:** Blue Line

The dominant trend line on the daily chart. Born from agulhadas on Oct 15, 2023 and Aug 2, 2024. Represents the primary daily trend.

> *""Tivemos 22 dias de férias acima da linha Azul""*

### EMA231

Exponential Moving Average of 231 periods. Used as a long-term trend filter on the daily chart. Acts as support/resistance.

> *""343 fechou MAIS UMA VEZ acima da EMA231""*

### RSI 43.83 / 71.52

Specific RSI levels used as cycle inflection points. 43.83 = bottom confirmation (buy). 71.52 = top confirmation (sell). These are unique to his analysis, not standard RSI levels.

> *""RSI 71.52: Confirmação de topo / RSI 43.82: Confirmação de fundo""*
